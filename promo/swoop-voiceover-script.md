# Swoop reels: voiceover script

Two reads, one per reel. Each line is timed to the scene it plays over, so the words land with the picture.
Start each line at its time; the window says how long you have. A calm, confident read at about 2.5 words a
second fits every window. Leave the gaps silent: the music and sound effects carry them.

Record each reel as one take, starting at 0.0 s of the video (a click or a count-in before it is fine; tell
me the offset). Save as `promo/swoop-chrome-voiceover.mp3` and `promo/swoop-windows-voiceover.mp3`, and I'll
mix it in: the music ducks under your voice automatically, then the MP4s are re-muxed without re-rendering.

## Reel 1: Swoop for Chrome (23 s)

**Tone:** low, unhurried, a little understated. A launch film, not an explainer.

| Start | Window | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 2.1 s | Not just the screen. | A screenshot only sees one screen. |
| 2.7 | 3.6 s | The whole page. | Swoop takes the whole page. *(beat)* Top to bottom, in one click. |
| 6.9 | 3.0 s | One tall image. | Every screen, joined into one picture. |
| 10.3 | 4.9 s | Mark it up. | Mark it up. *(beat)* Arrows, steps, blur. *(beat)* Every tool free, no watermark. |
| 15.7 | 2.8 s | Nothing is uploaded. | And nothing is uploaded. Ever. |
| 19.1 | 3.6 s | Swoop end card | Swoop. *(beat)* Full-page screenshots for Chrome. Free. |

Words: 50. Read straight through, it runs about 20 s.

## Every line is true

The lines say only what the reels already show, and the README backs each one: the whole page in one
image, one click, every editor tool free with no watermark, nothing uploaded, Print Screen opens Swoop and
freezes the screen (menus and tooltips stay put), Enter copies, a 1.4 MB installer, about 30 MB of memory
while it waits, free and open source. Please don't add speed claims or name other apps in the read.

## Optional alternative endings

- Chrome: "Swoop. Free, open source, and yours." (instead of the last line)
- Windows: "Swoop. The free Print Screen app for Windows."
- For X or Instagram you can add "Link below." / "Link in bio." after the last line. The reels are long
  enough for about 1 s more if the read runs over; tell me and I'll hold the end card a little longer.
